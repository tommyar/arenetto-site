A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING.

Before your first note.

Need a hand?
We’re here to help. ↗

Is Arenetto free?+

Yes. Arenetto is free on the App Store and Google Play, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Where can I play it?+

On iPhone and iPad, and on Android phones and tablets. The instrument is designed to be played in landscape. Check your store listing for current device compatibility.

What is push/pull control?+

A diatonic accordion plays different notes depending on bellows direction. Arenetto lets you select push or pull while playing the buttons.

Can I choose my layout and tuning?+

Yes. Choose 28, 31 or 34 buttons, select your sound family and tuning, and show note labels with sharps or flats if you prefer. Tablets also have button-size and spacing presets.

Do I need to create an account?+

No. There is no sign-up. Instrument preferences are saved locally on your device.

Is it available in Spanish?+

Yes. The app and this website are available in English and Spanish.
